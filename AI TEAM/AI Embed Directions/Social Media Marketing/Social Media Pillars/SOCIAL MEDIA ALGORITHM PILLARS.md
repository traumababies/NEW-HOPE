SOCIAL MEDIA ALGORITHM PILLARS



When building a brand and feeding the algorithm, you do not use thousands of categories like an advertiser does. As a brand, you focus on **3 to 5 content pillars** (or subject categories) at a time. 



Social media algorithms are machine-learning engines. If a brand posts about too many random subjects, the algorithm gets confused and doesn't know who to show the content to. 



The exact categories you listed earlier (Art History, Natural History, Vintage Film, Cosmetic Science, Business History, Literature, Holidays, and ASMR Texture) actually map beautifully into the standard 4-pillar brand subject framework used by modern social media teams: 



🏛️ The **4 Brand Content Pillars**



Every category you have can be grouped under one of these four master subjects to keep your brand focused:



Pillar 1: 



**Authority \& Education** (Builds Trust)



Your Categories: 

Organic ingredients vs. Synthetic ingredients, Chemistry Science, \& 

Historical Beauty Ingredients/Culture.



Why: This positions your brand as an expert. You are teaching your audience the history and science behind what you make. 



Pillar 2: 



**Brand Identity \& Storytelling** (Builds Connection)



Your Categories: 

Evolue Products and Ingredients in them, Evolue History, \& 

Hollywood/Vintage Film Philosophy.



Why: This gives your brand a soul. It explains why the brand exists and connects your products to a grander aesthetic or philosophy. 



Pillar 3: 



**Entertainment \& Culture** (Builds Reach/Shareability)



Your Categories: 

Holidays/Pop Culture 



Why: This content is highly shareable. It hops on seasonal trends and cultural moments (like Singles Day or the Autumn Festival) to pull in new followers who don't know your brand yet.

**Aesthetic \& Sensory Experience** (Builds Engagement)

🤖 How the Algorithm Reads Your Brand Subjects



Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube look at your last 9 to 12 posts to bucket your profile into a specific "niche". By grouping your 9 specific interests into these 4 clear strategic pillars, the machine-learning system will easily recognize your brand as a Premium, Heritage-Driven Cosmetic \& Lifestyle account, sending your posts directly to users who already watch similar content.
