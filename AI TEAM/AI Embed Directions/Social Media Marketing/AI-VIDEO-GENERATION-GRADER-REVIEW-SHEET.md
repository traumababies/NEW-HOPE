# AI Video Generation Grader Review Sheet

## Purpose

This grader independently evaluates whether an AI-generated proof or final shot satisfies the approved Creative Brief and is worth keeping. The grader must judge the actual generated media, not the prompt's intention.

The grader must not approve a shot merely because it looks attractive. It must prove that the shot performs its assigned production job.

# 1. Automatic blocking failures

Block the shot regardless of score if any applies:

- [ ] Required subject is absent.
- [ ] Required action is absent.
- [ ] Action is only implied, not visible.
- [ ] Action occurs too late to fit the timeline.
- [ ] Generated media is not the required duration or format.
- [ ] Important subject cannot survive platform crop.
- [ ] Major face, hand, body, product, or object defect appears.
- [ ] Generated text is broken or unintelligible when text is required.
- [ ] Product identity or packaging is visibly wrong.
- [ ] Unwanted person, logo, trademark, or private information appears.
- [ ] The shot contradicts the approved brief in a material way.
- [ ] Continuity makes the shot unusable.
- [ ] Rights or provenance record is missing.
- [ ] Proof was skipped for a high-risk final generation.
- [ ] Credit authorization was missing.
