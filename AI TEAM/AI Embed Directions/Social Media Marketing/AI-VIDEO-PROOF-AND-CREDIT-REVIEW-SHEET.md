# 4. Final authorization

Final generation may proceed only when:

- [ ] Proof passed.
- [ ] No critical defect remains.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are satisfied.
- [ ] Fallback is documented.
- [ ] Credit budget is acceptable.

- [ ] Owner or authorized reviewer approved the proof.
