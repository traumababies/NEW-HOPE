# Copy Grading Rubric and Review Protocol

## Purpose

This rubric defines how copy is graded after the media is complete and before captions, hashtags, disclosures, and alt text are approved for publishing.

The grader must judge the copy against the actual media, approved weekly theme, catalog evidence, platform requirements, owner taste, and applicable disclosure rules.

## Grading sequence

1. Inspect the completed media.
2. Read the approved Creative Brief.
3. Read the Cataloger's evidence record.
4. Read the Copywriter's TikTok and Instagram versions.
5. Check factual, product, rights, and disclosure issues.
6. Score each category.
7. Apply automatic-failure rules.
8. Return precise revisions.
9. Do not silently rewrite the copy.

## Score interpretation

- **0:** Missing, inaccurate, or unusable.
- **1:** Very weak.
- **2:** Weak.
- **3:** Acceptable.
- **4:** Strong.
- **5:** Excellent.

## Scoring categories

### Media fidelity — 20 points

Check whether the copy accurately describes what is visible or audible. Penalize invented actions, people, places, dates, events, motives, product results, or backstory.

### Hook quality — 15 points

Check whether the first line is specific, quickly understandable, curious without being misleading, and grounded in the actual media.

### Theme and narrative alignment — 15 points

Check whether the copy supports the weekly theme, the post's narrative role, emotional direction, and relationship to the other eight posts.

### Voice and readability — 15 points

Check grammar, clarity, scanability, brand voice, audience fit, and avoidance of corporate or generic filler.

### Value and CTA — 10 points

Check whether the copy adds context or value and uses one natural, appropriate action prompt.

### Platform adaptation — 10 points

Check whether TikTok and Instagram versions are adapted appropriately rather than copied without thought.

### Hashtag quality — 10 points

Check accuracy, relevance, niche usefulness, theme alignment, brand authorization, verification of location/events, and absence of stuffing.

### Accessibility, disclosure, and attribution — 5 points

Check alt text, visible-text description, commercial disclosure, attribution, and privacy.

## Automatic-failure rules

Block copy regardless of total score when it:

- Invents a material fact.
- Makes unsupported health, scientific, financial, product, or performance claims.
- Hides required affiliate, sponsorship, gifted-product, or partnership disclosure.
- Exposes private personal information.
- Uses deceptive clickbait.
- Claims personal use, expertise, endorsement, or results without verification.
- Uses inaccurate product ingredients or benefits.
- Misstates rights or attribution.
- Contains a serious factual, legal, or grammatical error.

## Required grader output

The grader must return:

- Total score.
- Category scores.
- Evidence for each score.
- Automatic-failure flags.
- Exact problematic sentence, hashtag, or field.
- Required revision.
- Fact-check queue.
- Rights/disclosure queue.
- Final status.

Allowed final statuses:

- `PASS`
- `PASS_WITH_WARNINGS`
- `RETURN_TO_COPYWRITER`
- `FACT_CHECK_REQUIRED`
- `RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `BLOCKED_FROM_PUBLISHING`
