# Grader Return Packet (Complete)

## Purpose

When a grader rejects or revises work, the Creator (Creative Director for
briefs, Copywriter for copy) must respond with **numbered fixes, not
feelings**. It carries: verdict → numbered fix list → per-fix decision →
re-grade → revision bump.

**Rules that always apply:**

- A grader's fix list is a **contract**. Every numbered item is either fixed
  (with evidence) or declined (with an owner-approved reason). No third option.
- The Creator never grades, approves, or silently rewrites her own work.
- Every resubmission bumps the revision number by 1.
- **Max 2 redo cycles per standing rule.** A third requires the owner's
  password override, recorded in section G.
- Declined fixes quote the owner's decision.
