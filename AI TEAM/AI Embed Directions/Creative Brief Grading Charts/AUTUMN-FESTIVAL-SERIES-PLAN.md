## Post 1 detail — *Balsam Nails at Chuseok* (Jean's memory)

**The tradition:** Korean Chuseok balsam manicure (봉숭아 물들이기). Girls crush
garden-balsam petals (Impatiens balsamina) with leaves + a little alum, wrap
fingers overnight, and wake to natural orange-red-stained nails. A harvest-
moon ritual of youth, tied to the festival.

**Jean's story thread:** little Jean, Chuseok at the family home, crushing the
flowers + leaves, winding thread around her small fingers, going to sleep and
waking to the stain. The nail-stain as the festival's first you-wear-it mark.

**Visual direction:**
- Warm varandas/courtyard Chuseok light; a small girl's hands, garden balsam
  petals + green leaves crushed into a soft paste in a little stone bowl
- Fingers wrapped in leaf + thread; the twirl and tie (macro, deliberate)
- Cut to the morning: nails stained soft orange-red, proud little-girl hands
- Palette: garden balsam's pink/rose → the orange-red stain; plum + amber
  evening, a hint of the rising harvest moon
- Feel: tender, specific, a memory — not a beauty ad

**Audio:** gentle; a distant festival-night hum, the swish of crushing petals,
a soft chime as the moon rises. No rush.

**Voiceover (if any):** Jean's memory, warm + a little wistful — "When I was
little, at Chuseok…" — not promotional.

## Oat-flour note (applies to ALL posts)

The week's signature material is **oat flour — creamy, milk-powder pale-beige
(not brown dirt, not stark white)**. In every post it appears, render it as
soft, powdery, dust-like, milky-cream light. It dusts the harvest textures,
swirls in the lantern light, and (in Post 6) becomes the whole storm. Owners
of any post should include this exact oat-flour language in their prompt.
