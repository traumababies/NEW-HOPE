# Creative Grader Review Sheet

## Purpose

The Creative Grader evaluates artistic quality, emotional coherence, theme fidelity, nine-post storytelling, visual identity, audio identity, originality, and alignment with the owner's taste.

The Creative Director must not grade her own brief.

Score each item from 0 to 5:

- **0** = absent or unusable
- **1** = very weak
- **2** = weak
- **3** = acceptable
- **4** = strong
- **5** = exceptional
