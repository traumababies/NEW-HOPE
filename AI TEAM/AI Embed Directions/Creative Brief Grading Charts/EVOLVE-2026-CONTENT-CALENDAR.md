# Evolve 2026 Content Calendar — the year-long rhythm

Four seasons, each a **9-post themed week** (one IPTC subject per post, no
repeats), all carrying the **oat-flour signature** (creamy pale-beige,
milk-powder light) as the house material. Every week lands as a Mon/Wed/Fri
× 3 drop, ending on the season's big night.

The arc: **winter = warmth of light · spring = renewal · summer = the sun
ritual · autumn = harvest & the turn (Festival → Exfoliate → Halloween)**.
