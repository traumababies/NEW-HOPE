# Weekly Creative Brief Template

## Purpose

The Creative Director must complete this template every week before Content Scout, Muse, Studio, or Media Editor work begins.

This document is a proposal only. Completing it does not approve sourcing, generation, editing, publishing, or library changes.

The brief must be specific enough that:

- Content Scout can search without guessing.
- Muse can match approved library media to exact needs.
- Studio can compare candidates against objective acceptance criteria.
- Media Editor can assemble or generate the post without inventing missing direction.
- Brief Validator can check completeness and timing.
- Creative Grader can evaluate the idea and continuity.
- Production Grader can evaluate feasibility, risk, rights, and credit exposure.
