# EVOLVE 2026 — Full-Year 9-Post Content Calendar

**House material:** oat flour — creamy, milk-powder pale-beige, soft powdery
dust (NOT brown, NOT stark white). It's the signature light in every series.

**Structure:** each month/week = exactly one 9-post series (one IPTC subject
per post, no repeats), dropping Mon / Wed / Fri × 3, ending on the season's
big night.

**The arc:** winter = warmth of light · spring = renewal · summer = the sun
ritual · autumn = the turn (Festival → Exfoliate → Halloween) — the year's
peak.
