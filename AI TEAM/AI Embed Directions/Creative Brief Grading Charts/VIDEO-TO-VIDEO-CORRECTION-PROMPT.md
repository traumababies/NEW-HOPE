## Tips that save you the most money

- **Use video-to-video / reference mode**, NOT text-to-video — much cheaper
  (less to infer) and more faithful to your clip.
- **Attach the source clip + 2–3 face photos**, not all 5 — enough likeness,
  less processing.
- **Keep duration = source (21s)** or slightly less (16s) — longer = pricier.
- **Set motion low / style-preserving** if the tool offers it.
- **Preview a short segment first** (if offered) before committing full res —
  catches "flour too dark / face still off" cheaply.
